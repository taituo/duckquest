🦆 DuckQuest Tracker

Connecting to the Steam Deck…
  1. Press Start tracking and allow the immersive session.
  2. You will see passthrough. Take the headset off (sensor between the lenses taped over).
  3. Place it under the TV facing the players and don't move it again.
  4. On the TV: press A/X on a controller (or C on the Deck) to calibrate.

If you long-press the Meta button (recenter) or the boundary resets, calibrate again.
