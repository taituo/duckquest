# 04b — Physics stickman brawler

Inspired by physics stickman fighting games such as Landfall's *Stick Fight: The Game*.

## Why it suits motion control

- Floppy ragdoll characters: your real hand drives the stickman's arm or weapon directly. Swing wildly and it flails wildly.
- Doesn't need precision to be fun; clumsiness is part of the joke and hides tracking jitter.
- Side view, so the same mapping and mirroring as [04](04-side-view-sword-duel.md).

## Controls

- Controller position → weapon hand, physics pulls the body along
- Thumbstick → walk, button → jump
- Swing speed → hit strength
