# 09 — Controller on the head

Use the two controllers for different jobs: **one on your head, one in your hand**.

- **Head controller** on a cap or headband, front face toward the Quest → eye position for the window effect ([05](05-tv-as-window.md)).
- **Hand controller** → gun, sword or tool, with all the buttons.

## Trade-offs

- One-player setup (both controllers are yours).
- The head controller's tracking lights (front face) must point toward the Quest.
- Its buttons are out of reach.
- It sits a few cm above the eyes; a one-time calibration corrects that.

## Other combinations

One on the chest for body position, one in the hand. The game needs a setting for which controller has which role.
