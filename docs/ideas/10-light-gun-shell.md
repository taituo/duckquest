# 10 — 3D-printed light gun and huge screen

## Gun shell

- The controller sits inside the shell; the shell's trigger presses the real trigger.
- **Keep the tracking lights uncovered** (mostly on the controller's front face), as ready-made Quest gun stocks do.
- Any shape works: the bullseye calibration step corrects for the angle the controller sits at.
- Printable models and ready-made pistol grips for Quest controllers exist; good starting point before designing one.

## Huge screen

Screen size doesn't matter; calibration just measures where the corners are. A projector on the wall works. Bigger screen = bigger targets = easier, more fun.

Wall-sized ducks, a plastic gun, ducking behind the sofa: an arcade at home.
