# 13 — Without a Quest: phone or webcam

Rougher alternatives if a Quest isn't available:

- **Phone as a pointer**: its motion sensors through a web page. Feels like a Wii remote, but the aim slowly drifts.
- **Webcam hand tracking**: cheap, but less precise and slower than the Quest.
- **Mouse**: DuckQuest already supports it for testing.

The Quest remains by far the most accurate option.
