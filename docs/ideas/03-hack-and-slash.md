# 03 — Hack-and-slash

A dungeon hack-and-slash (in the spirit of any existing game in the genre) where the controller is your weapon.

## What the tracking gives

- Full position and angle of both controllers
- Swing speed (the tracker page already sends the grip's linear velocity) → slash direction and power
- Second hand as shield or off-hand weapon

## Placement

On top of the TV tilted down, or chest-mounted (see [placement](../placement.md)).
