# 05 — TV as a window (head-coupled perspective)

When the game knows where your eyes are, it redraws the 3D scene so the TV looks like a real window. Lean left and you see further around the right edge; crouch and you look up into the scene. You literally peek around corners.

Known technique: Johnny Lee showed it in 2007 with a Wii remote and lights on safety glasses.

## Getting the head position

- A controller on a cap or headband (see [09](09-controller-on-head.md)), or
- A chest-mounted Quest (body position, see [placement](../placement.md))

## Movement

Your idea: **hold a button to move** the character; release it to stay still and just lean or crouch to look around.

## Catch

The 3D effect only looks right for the tracked person. Others see a moving picture without depth.

## First prototype

A small 3D room behind the TV, the other controller as a flashlight or gun. Also the best test of whether tracking is smooth enough for the bigger games.
