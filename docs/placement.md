# Where to place the Quest

| Placement | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Under the TV, facing the players** | Shooting | Controllers point at the cameras, the side they track best. Chest height, tilted up 10–15°. |
| **Facing you, TV off to the side** | Seated play | Works with aim calibration (point at corners from your seat). If the crosshair jumps, turn the Quest slightly toward the TV. |
| **On top of the TV, tilted down** | Sword games | Catches overhead swings and crouching better. |
| **Strapped to the chest** | Body tracking, sword games | The Quest's own position = your body position (lean, crouch, walk). Blind spots behind the back and above the head. One Quest per player. Warm, ~500 g. |
| **Overhead boom stand** | Two players side by side | Tilt about **45° toward the players**, not straight down. Mic / ring-light / overhead-filming boom stands work. |
| **Ceiling hook** | Later / advanced | Swings and spins, needs power up there, may be far from the players. Add a safety cord; keep away from where kids sleep or play. |

## General rules

- Players **1.5–2.5 m** from the Quest.
- **Fixed in place**: if it moves after calibrating, recalibrate.
- **Even lighting**: no bright window behind the players, no mirrors in view.
- **Tape the proximity sensor** (see [keep-awake.md](keep-awake.md)).
- Calibration doesn't care where the Quest is, as long as it can see the controllers while you play.
